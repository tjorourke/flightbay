Why this is calmer than v1

  1. Four questions, not 20 rows. Each card answers one question in a sentence and lists only what is configured underneath. The answer cards and the table from v1 were saying the same thing twice; now the answer is the heading.
  2. Things that are not set are links, not rows. "Add" at the bottom of each card holds the tasks for that area (short labels, no descriptions). "More…" opens the full list. Unavailable tasks are one muted link.
  3. One recommendation, inside the card it belongs to. The bypass warning sits under the rule it weakens.
  4. Detail is one click away. Enforced by, Managed by, layer (L4/L7) and status move to the drawer and object page. The resource name stays, small and grey, so you can still recognise it.
  5. Multicluster is one line at the bottom, only shown when the clusters differ.
  6. Locked tasks stay visible. Rate limiting and retries show greyed with a lock and a one-line reason, so you can see they exist and why you cannot use them on this waypoint.
  7. Summary | All tasks. Summary (this view) is the default. All tasks shows the full catalogue of 20 tasks grouped under the same four questions, as cards or a list (see 01c and 01d).
