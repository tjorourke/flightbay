Why this page

  1. "Applies to" in words. The scope Flightbay already computes (targetRefs, selector, namespace, root namespace) becomes a column, so you never have to read targetRefs to know what a policy hits. Broken targets are flagged.
  2. One column per connected cluster. Drift shows straight away: the split and the DENY exist in kind-mesh1 only. "Differs" opens a field diff. Uses the live mirror, so it costs nothing extra.
  3. Managed by. Read from managedFields / labels (Flightbay, Helm, Argo CD, kubectl). This tells you whether editing in Flightbay will be fought by GitOps.
  4. Covers the ambient kinds only: AuthorizationPolicy, PeerAuthentication, RequestAuthentication, HTTPRoute, DestinationRule, waypoint Gateways and agentgateway policies on waypoints. Clicking a group header opens that Service in Ambient. Replaces the per-Service "Existing configuration" table hidden in the Attachment map.
